Everybody makes silly mistakes. And we should all do our best not to judge
other people for making their own silly mistakes. My silly mistake ended up
with me deleting my entire Bluesky account. So now I have to start anew, with
zero followers and zero history. Oh well, whatever.

Oh, but first, please help me rebuild by following me at
[bsky.app/mpadge.eu](https://bsky.app/profile/mpadge.eu)!

<div class="info-block">

In trying to set up my new [whole life on atproto](/notes/atproto.html), I
wanted to move my primary data from BlueSky to somewhere else. Just because I
could. The somewhere else was [tangled.org](https://tangled.org), for
atproto-empowered code hosting. I mucked up one step, and wanted to delete a
connection I had made between Bluesky and tangled. Clicked a "Delete" button,
entered a confirmation code, and ... deleted the whole record. Whoops.
<br><br>
Everybody makes silly mistakes.
