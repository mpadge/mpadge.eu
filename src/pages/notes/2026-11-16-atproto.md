This new "Notes" sub-page of my site is a part of my restructuring of all of my
online life and presence through [atproto](https://atproto.com). Everything on
this blog can be viewed through any `site.standard` reader, such as
[`standard-reader.app`](https://standard-reader.app/search?q=mpadge.eu).

I believe in the potential of [atproto](https://atproto.com), and am going to
try to design as much of my online life as I can around it. I also recently
lost my job, and so have a very concrete first goal: To try to find work
entirely via [atproto](https://atproto.com).

To try to help anybody else who wants to set themselves a similar goal, I am
going to document the entire process as much as I can. I have also made an
entirely fresh start by erasing my previous [Bluesky](https://bsky.app)
profile[^profile]. So I have no followers, and am truly and literally starting from
scratch.

[^profile]: It was actually an accident. I'll write more about that later.
    Everybody makes silly mistakes sometimes. This is just one I am going to
    take advantage of.

announcements at [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profiles/mpadge.eu), to full

All [atproto](https://atproto.com), every time, every place. Let see where this
can go!
